01 · The problem

Single-model trading systems fail
in four predictable ways.

  1. Non-stationarity. A strategy tuned on a quiet bull tape is not merely weaker in a liquidity crisis - it is solving a different problem. With no explicit regime variable, the wrong policy is applied silently.
  2. False consensus. Ensembling models that share a feature set produces agreement that looks like confidence and is actually correlation.
  1. Risk applied after the fact. Sizing bolted downstream of a "buy" cannot prevent a structurally bad trade. It can only make it smaller.
  2. Unfalsifiable claims. A backtest curve is not evidence. A system that never reports its own rejected trades is not auditable.

02 · The thesis

The market is not one thing - and no single
model should be trusted to say what it is.

Regime before opportunity

An HMM classifies the environment into twelve archetypes, R01 to R12. Every downstream component is conditioned on that classification.

Disagreement stays visible

Seven agents view the same market through seven lenses. Their disagreement is computed and carried forward as a first-class quantity, never averaged away.

The core rule
Gate capital,
not opinion.

A seven-state gate sits between signal and broker. It can ALLOW, REDUCE, BLOCK or FLATTEN - on measured capacity, not on model confidence.

04 · Stage one

Classify the environment before
you evaluate the opportunity.

A bounded feature vector - momentum, realised volatility via ATR, a VIX proxy, volume profile - is mapped by a Hidden Markov Model onto an affinity distribution over twelve regime archetypes.

The affinities are normalised to sum to 1.0. That property is not asserted in prose; it is enforced by a Hypothesis-generated property test over randomised price and volume series.

Downstream effect

Regime selects the portfolio construction method: mean-variance in R01 and R11, risk-parity in R03 and R05, minimum-variance in the stress regimes R04 and R07.

06 · Stage three

Disagreement is data,
not noise to be averaged out.

The fused signal is a reputation-weighted mean of the seven directions. Alongside it, the platform computes a disagreement statistic over the same seven inputs.

High disagreement does not veto a trade on its own. It propagates into the capital gate, where it reduces available capacity - the system trades smaller precisely when its own analysts are least aligned.

Bounded by construction

Signal, disagreement and confidence are all proven bounded for randomised seven-agent inputs by property-based tests, not by spot-checked examples.

07 · Stage four

Influence has to be earned,
and it can be lost.

Every agent carries a Beta-Binomial posterior over its own realised hit rate, updated on each reconciled outcome.

  1. An agent that has been right is weighted up.
  2. An agent that has been wrong is weighted down.
  3. A new agent starts at the prior and has to earn its influence before it can move capital.

Reputation state is persisted and restored across sessions, so the learning loop survives a process restart rather than resetting to a flattering blank slate.

Update rule

αₓ ← αₓ + 1  on a correct call
βₓ  ← βₓ + 1  on an incorrect call
wₓ  ∝  αₓ / (αₓ + βₓ)

The posterior mean is the weight. Uncertainty shrinks only with evidence, so a lucky streak of two does not buy the same influence as a track record of two hundred.

08 · Stage five

Smooth the signal, keep the covariance honest.

The fused signal is treated as a noisy observation of a latent two-dimensional state - level and trend - and smoothed by a linear Kalman filter.

The covariance update uses the Joseph form, which preserves symmetry and positive-definiteness under finite precision where the naive form does not.

10 · Why a product, not a sum

A strong macro backdrop cannot buy permission
to trade through a liquidity hole.

If the gate summed

Six healthy dimensions would out-vote one catastrophic dimension. The system would size into a crisis because the average looked fine.

Because the gate multiplies

A single depleted dimension drives the composite factor toward zero regardless of the other six. Capacity is a conjunction, not a popularity contest.

Ten discrete rules sit on top

CONC-001 through EXEC-001 cover concentration, leverage, drawdown, sector exposure, liquidity and execution timeout.

Verdict Effect on the order
ALLOWProceeds at the computed cap
REDUCEProceeds at a reduced notional
BLOCKNo order is submitted
FLATTENExisting exposure is closed

Every rejection is written to the audit trail with the citation of the rule that caused it. A blocked trade is as visible on the dashboard as an executed one.

11 · Execution

Paper endpoints only, behind a breaker.

  1. Product gate. A surviving decision is routed to an instrument - equity, options or cash - according to regime and the smoothed signal.
  2. Alpaca paper submission. The execution layer contacts only the paper endpoint. Live-money trading is out of scope at the architecture level, not merely by configuration.
  3. Emergency circuit breaker. A single call cancels all open orders and closes all positions, with polling until the account is confirmed flat.
  4. Reconciliation. Fills are compared against expectation, and the outcome updates both agent reputation and trade memory.
Order safety

Preview, notional check and buying-power check before any submission.

Idempotency

Client order IDs so an ambiguous retry cannot duplicate an order.

Audit trail

Every decision, allowed or rejected, is appended with its rule citation.

Hard boundary
No live money

No custody, no client funds, no regulated advice.

The honest slide.

No performance figures

No returns, no Sharpe, no win rate. None has been independently verified, so none appears anywhere in this submission.

No live-money trading

This is a demonstration of decision architecture and risk discipline, not of profitability.

Coverage is 82 percent, not 100

The uncovered surfaces - dashboard callback branches, HMM calibration internals, live-session error paths - are enumerated explicitly in the engineering report rather than papered over.

Mutation testing configured, not executed

The tool requires WSL, unavailable on the build machine. The configuration is committed. The score is not fabricated.
